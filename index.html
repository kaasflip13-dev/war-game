📜 RULES

Speel eerlijk, heb plezier, en respecteer andere piloten.

🎯 Fair Play Don't use cheats, macros, bots or exploits to artificially boost your score, credits or leaderboard position.
📡 Leaderboard When you log in, your name, profile photo and best score become publicly visible on the leaderboard for all players.
💾 Cloud Save Your progress is automatically saved to the cloud once you're logged in, so you can keep playing on any device.
🙊 Respect Don't choose offensive or fake names. Names considered offensive may be removed from the leaderboard.
🔒 Privacy Log out on shared devices. Your gameplay stats aren't sensitive, but your account is still yours.

These rules keep SpaceBots Ultra fun and fair for everyone. Play smart, climb the leaderboard, and defeat the bosses! 🤖
